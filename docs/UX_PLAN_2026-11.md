## The core idea

Cutely Ask Me is one date told from two sides, but today it feels like two separate apps glued together.
The person invited gets a delightful, focused, mobile-first wizard.
The sender gets a power tool with about 60 controls above the fold, and neither side can see where the date stands.

The proposal is to organize everything around **one shared "date thread"** with five visible moments: **Asked, Picked, Planned, Shared, Memory**.
Both sides see the same thread, every screen shows exactly one next action, and the sender's planner becomes a guided "one stop at a time" flow that reads like the wizard the person invited already loves.
The brand, colors, type and playful voice stay; the organization of content and the flow change.

## Problems by screen

Evidence references the review screenshots, copied into [`docs/ux-2026-11/before/`](./ux-2026-11/before/).

### Home (`/`)

- The page is one heading ("Date Night"), one sentence and two buttons that both lead to sign-in ([before/home-desktop](./ux-2026-11/before/home-desktop.png), [before/home-phone](./ux-2026-11/before/home-phone.png)).
- Nothing shows the product: no ask, no plan, no keepsake, and the name on the page is not the product name.
- A visitor cannot tell who it is for or why to sign up.

### Sender dashboard (`/dashboard`)

- The first thing a new sender sees is a notification permission card, then two grey lines ("No invites yet.", "No past dates yet.") ([before/dashboard-desktop](./ux-2026-11/before/dashboard-desktop.png)).
- Once dates exist, the same date appears in up to three places ("Up next", "Sent invites", "Your map + stats") with different labels ("completed", "Date with Jamie") ([before/dashboard-phone](./ux-2026-11/before/dashboard-phone.png)).
- A status pill like "completed" describes the invite, not the date, and there is no "what do I do next" for each date.
- On desktop the whole page is a 480px column in a 1440px window.

### Sender planner (`/plan/[id]`)

- About 60 buttons sit above the fold: start and pickup addresses, search center, radius slider, API call counter, price chips and duration chips on every stop ([before/planner-desktop](./ux-2026-11/before/planner-desktop.png), [before/planner-phone](./ux-2026-11/before/planner-phone.png)).
- The real job, "pick a place for each stop", starts below the fold on phone, after five setup sections.
- Search results all looked like "★ 5.0 · $$", so they did not help choose.
- Before the fix, the share buttons sat under a sticky map and could end up off screen; the `fix/basics` pull request moved them under the stops, but there is still no overall sense of progress.
- Copy assumes a female recipient ("Her vibe", "Surprise her").

### The ask and the wizard (`/invite/[token]`)

- The ask is the strongest screen in the product, but on desktop the avatar bubble floats in the far corner and the buttons sit off-centre in the card ([before/ask-desktop](./ux-2026-11/before/ask-desktop.png)).
- Step buttons mix casing ("continue", "open Cute Cal", "Back", "Almost there →") and the six progress hearts have no labels ([before/wizard-phone](./ux-2026-11/before/wizard-phone.png)).
- The summary says "It's a date." before anything is locked in, and the next screen says "It's a date!" again.

### After "Lock it in" and the shared plan (recipient)

- "Go to dashboard" is offered to someone without an account ([before/confirmed-phone](./ux-2026-11/before/confirmed-phone.png)).
- "See the plan" immediately says "Your date is a surprise" even though the sender has not decided anything yet, and the person cannot see what they just picked ([before/waiting-phone](./ux-2026-11/before/waiting-phone.png)).
- The shared plan has a lovely AI title but no map, no addresses, no directions, no calendar button and no dress vibe ([before/shared-plan-phone](./ux-2026-11/before/shared-plan-phone.png)).

### Cross-cutting

- Low contrast: helper text `#c0a0b0` is 2.2:1, white on the primary pink `#ff7aaa` is 2.4:1.
- Raw hex colors are repeated hundreds of times instead of tokens.
- Emoji-only controls (reorder arrows, verdicts, settings gear) lack visible labels.

## Principles

1. **One next action per screen.** Every screen answers "what do I do now?" with one primary button; everything else is secondary or tucked away.
2. **Show the thread.** Both sides see the same five moments (Asked, Picked, Planned, Shared, Memory) in the same words and order.
3. **Mobile first, desktop uses the space.** Design at 390px, then let desktop add a second column (the map, the thread) instead of stretching one column.
4. **Guide, then allow power.** Defaults that work (suggested places, sensible durations, detected timezone), with every advanced control one tap away under "Fine-tune".
5. **The person invited never needs an account** and never sees a dead end.
6. **Keep the voice, fix the words.** Playful sentence case everywhere, neutral pronouns or the person's name, no internal jargon (no API counters, no IANA ids).
7. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA contrast, 44px touch targets, labelled icon buttons, visible focus, and reduced-motion respected.
8. **Every state is designed.** Empty, loading, error, waiting and done states are part of the screen, not afterthoughts.

## Proposed structure and flow

### The date thread

| Moment | Sender sees | Person invited sees | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asked | "Waiting for Sam to answer" | The ask | Sam: say yes |
| Picked | "Sam picked Saturday, 6:30 PM" | "Waiting for Nate to pick the spots" with their own picks | Sender: pick the spots |
| Planned | "2 of 3 spots picked" | Same waiting state | Sender: finish and share |
| Shared | "Shared Nov 2" or "Surprise is set" | The full plan with map, addresses, directions, calendar | Both: add to calendar |
| Memory | "How did it go?" then the keepsake | Same | Both: rate, then keep the card |

The thread is a small five-dot strip with labels, reused on the dashboard card, the planner header and the recipient plan.

### Sender flow

1. **Home** to **Sign up** (email or Google, now working) to **Ask someone** (name, email, area).
2. **Invite ready** screen leads with "Copy the link and send it yourself", with email as a bonus, and a "See what they'll see" preview link.
3. **Dashboard** lists dates as cards, newest action first, each with the thread and one action button.
4. **Planner** (guided): a short "Sam picked" summary, then one card per stop with three suggestions, then a sticky "Share" bar that fills in as stops are picked.
5. **Shared** state: read-only plan with calendar, directions and "Cancel date" (already built in `fix/basics`).
6. **After the date**: rating, verdicts and keepsake, as today.

### Person invited flow

1. **The ask** (unchanged hook, centred, avatar anchored to the card).
2. **Wizard** with labelled progress ("Step 3 of 6: activities"), sentence-case buttons.
3. **"Look good?"** summary with the detected timezone as one quiet line (built in `fix/basics`) and "Lock it in".
4. **"It's a date!"** confirmation with "See your picks" instead of "Go to dashboard", and an optional "Get a text or email when it's planned".
5. **Waiting** screen: "Nate is picking the spots" with their own picks and the thread.
6. **The plan**: map, stops with addresses and "Directions", "Add to calendar", dress vibe and tips; or "It's a surprise" only when the sender chose that.

### Information architecture

- `/` public landing.
- `/dashboard` the sender's dates; `/send` asks someone; `/settings` user preferences only (API usage moves to an owner-only `/admin/usage`).
- `/plan/[id]` one URL, role-aware: guided planner or shared view for the sender, waiting or plan view for the person invited, reflection after the date.
- `/plan/[id]/memory` the keepsake.

## What changes per screen

### Home (prototype screen 1)

- Hero: "Ask them cutely. Plan it for real." with a live mini ask card (a dodging "No" and a "Yes") as the visual.
- One primary action, "Ask someone out", and a secondary "Try the demo" that opens the name-only invite labelled as a demo.
- "How it works" in three steps: You ask, They pick the vibe, You plan the real spots.
- A keepsake preview and a short footer; "Sign in" moves to the header.

### Dashboard (prototype screen 2)

- Each date is one card: name, date and time, the thread, and one action ("Pick the spots", "Waiting for Sam", "Share the plan", "Rate the night").
- Sections become "Needs you", "Coming up" and "Memories"; the duplicate "Sent invites" list and stat tiles fold into those cards.
- Empty state: an illustration, "No dates yet. Ask someone cute.", and the primary action.
- The notification prompt appears only after the first invite, as a slim dismissible banner.
- Desktop: cards in a two-column grid with the "Places you loved" map on the right.

### Planner (prototype screen 3)

- Header: "Plan your date with Sam", the thread, and a compact "Sam picked" row (day, time, vibe, weather).
- One card per stop, in order, each with three suggestions (photo or placeholder, name, distance, price, rating with review count) and "Pick", plus "More ways" (search, drop a pin, places you loved).
- A picked stop collapses to one line with the place and "Change".
- "Fine-tune" disclosure holds start and pickup addresses, search area, radius, durations and leg modes; the API counter is removed from user UI.
- Sticky bottom bar on phone, right column on desktop: "2 of 3 spots picked" with a checklist and "Share with Sam", disabled until ready (the gate already exists in `fix/basics`).
- The map moves to the right column on desktop and becomes a collapsible "Show map" strip on phone.

### The plan for the person invited (prototype screen 4)

- Waiting state: "Nate is picking the spots" with the thread and their own picks, so nothing feels lost.
- Shared state: title and summary, a map with numbered stops, stop cards with address and "Directions", "Add to calendar", dress vibe and tips.
- Surprise state: only the day, time and dress vibe, with "The rest is a surprise until 6:30 PM".

### The ask and wizard (small changes, no prototype)

- Centre the Yes and No pair, anchor the avatar bubble to the card on desktop.
- Sentence case on every button, labelled progress hearts, "Look good?" on the summary.
- After a few dodges, reveal a gentle "Maybe another time" that tells the sender kindly.

### Visual system

- Tokens in `globals.css` via Tailwind `@theme`: `ink #3a2530`, `muted #7a5a68` (5.7:1), `pink #ff7aaa` (decoration and fills with ink text), `pink-strong #c93a73` (buttons with white text, 4.8:1), `lavender #7c5aa0`, `blush #fff7fb`.
- Replace the 132 raw `text-[#8a6a78]` and 106 `#c0a0b0` uses with tokens in one sweep.
- Buttons: three sizes (44, 48, 56px tall), sentence case, one primary style per screen.

## What stays

- The name, Cutely Ask Me, the Nunito type and the pink, blush and lavender palette.
- The dodging "No", the stickers, the floating hearts and confetti (respecting reduced motion).
- The wizard steps and their copy, apart from casing.
- The deterministic itinerary with Gemini only writing notes, tips, titles and poems.
- The keepsake card and its share link.
- Security and privacy choices: recipient capability cookies, strict CSP, no account needed for the person invited.

## Landing page

A concept landing page in the style of the assetarsenal concept pages, at `cutelyaskme.n8builds.dev` now and `cutelyask.me` once the domain is bought.

### What it says

- Headline: "Ask them cutely. Plan it for real."
- Subhead: "Send a cute invite with a No button that runs away. They pick the vibe, you plan the real spots, and you both keep the memory."
- Three steps: "You ask" (a personal link, no app to install), "They pick" (day, time, two activities, food, dress vibe), "You plan" (real places on a map, travel times, calendar, share or keep it a surprise).
- Keepsake section: "After the date, rate the night and get a little poem and photo card to keep."
- Why it is different: other ask pages stop at "Yes", and planners are single-player; this is the handoff between the two.
- Call to action: "Join the waitlist" (email only) plus "Try the demo ask" linking to `/invite/YourName` labelled as a demo.
- Footer: "A small project by Nathan Watkins", privacy note ("we only email you about launch").

## Demo path

A five-minute walkthrough for a demo or a reviewer:

1. Open `/` and read the hero, then tap "Try the demo" to open `/invite/YourName` and tap "No" a few times.
2. Say yes and walk the wizard to "Look good?", noting the detected timezone line.
3. Sign up with email at `/sign-up` and send a real invite to yourself from `/send`.
4. Open the invite link in a private window, finish the wizard and lock it in, then open "See your picks".
5. Back as the sender, open the date from the dashboard, pick a place for each stop, watch the share bar fill, and share.
6. Reload as the person invited to see the plan with map, directions and calendar.
7. For the post-date flow, use a plan dated in the past and open the keepsake.

## Rollout plan

Each step is its own pull request with screenshots, and each waits for the owner's approval.

1. Tokens and contrast sweep (no layout changes).
2. Recipient waiting and shared plan views (smallest, highest value for the person invited).
3. Dashboard cards with the thread.
4. Guided planner, keeping the current planner behind "Fine-tune" until the new one is proven.
5. Home and landing page with the waitlist.
6. Wizard polish (casing, labels, "Look good?", gentle decline).
